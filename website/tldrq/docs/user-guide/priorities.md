# Priorities & Read Status

Every bookmark has a priority and a read state. Together they tell you what to read next.

## The four levels

| Level | Left border on the card | Badge |
|---|---|---|
| Urgent | Red | A red "Urgent" badge |
| High | Orange | An orange "High" badge |
| Normal (the default) | None, or blue while unread | No badge |
| Low | Gray | A gray "Low" badge |

## Setting a priority

- **When saving**: choose a level in the priority menu next to **Save**. It goes back to Normal after each save.
- **Later**: open the bookmark's ⋮ menu, choose **Set priority**, and pick a level. The current level has a ✓.

Bulk imports apply one priority to every imported link (see
[Import, Export & Bulk Actions](import-export.md#import-the-file)).

## Filtering and sorting by priority

In **Filters**:

- The **Priority:** row shows **All**, **Urgent**, **High**, **Normal** or **Low** bookmarks.
- The **Sort:** row orders the list **Newest first** (the default) or **By priority**: Urgent, then High, Normal and
  Low, newest first within each level.

## Read and unread

New bookmarks are unread. Each card has a button to change that: **Mark as read** (blue) on an unread bookmark,
**Mark as unread** (gray) on a read one. Opening the link doesn't change it; you decide when you're done with a
bookmark.

The **Filter:** row in **Filters** shows **All**, **Unread** or **Read** bookmarks.

## How a card shows both

A Normal bookmark has a blue left border while it's unread and no border once it's read. Urgent, High and Low
bookmarks always show their priority's color instead, so on those cards the button tells you the read state:
**Mark as read** means it's still unread.

[Share links](sharing.md) show priority borders and badges, but not read state.
